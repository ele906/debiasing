# Visual spot checks

Two comparison grids were inspected: requested-present Indian-man and Hispanic-woman prompts, seed 1000. These are spot checks, not a complete independent annotation pass.

- At target alpha 0, necklaces remain visible in both examples. Their shape/thickness changes relative to alpha 1.
- Face appearance and pose also change across strengths; the intervention affects more than the necklace region.
- The random control also changes the portrait and necklace appearance. It is not established as semantically neutral.

All grids use identical prompts and fresh seeded noise streams. Alpha 1 was checked against baseline pixel-for-pixel. No best alpha has been selected, and the experiment does not demonstrate reduced racism.
